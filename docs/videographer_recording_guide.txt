================================================================================
PRAVAH FRONTEND MVP DEMO - VIDEOGRAPHER RECORDING GUIDE & WALKTHROUGH SCRIPT
================================================================================

This document details the exact visual cues, camera focus points, and narration
scripts required to record a high-quality demonstration video of the PRAVAH
AI-driven energy supply chain resilience platform.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRODUCTION TIPS FOR THE VIDEOGRAPHER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Capture Resolution: Record in standard 1080p (1920x1080) or 4K (3840x2160).
2. Browser Settings: Run the browser in full-screen mode (F11 in Chrome/Edge)
   to hide browser bars and bookmarks.
3. Mouse Movement: Move the cursor slowly, smoothly, and in straight lines.
   Pause for 1-2 seconds on hover targets to allow tooltips to fully render.
4. Screen Transitions: Ensure camera exposure and white balance adjust smoothly
   between the application's light themes and the premium dark themes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 1: ONBOARDING & THE CITIZEN VIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Visual Actions]
- Start on the landing page of the application (Citizen View / Public page).
- Allow the page to load and capture the animated Risk Index Gauge as it
  sweeps to its current value (e.g., 78/100).
- Hover over the simple SVG world map highlighting the critical maritime 
  corridors (Strait of Hormuz, Red Sea, Cape of Good Hope) connecting to India.
- Scroll down to show the published reference cards:
  * Brent Crude Price Reference
  * 88% Import Dependency (PPAC reference)
  * 9.5 Days Strategic Petroleum Reserve Cover (ISPRL reference)
- ACTION: Click the blue "Go deeper — Analyst View" button at the bottom of the
  page, or select "Analyst View" from the top-right header tier selector.

[Narration Script]
"We begin on the public Citizen View. This dashboard provides immediate 
transparency into India's crude import vulnerability, displaying a real-time 
Composite Risk Index and highlighting key corridors like the Strait of Hormuz 
and the Red Sea. We now transition to the Analyst View to inspect the active 
threat vectors."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 2: COMMAND CENTER (MAIN DASHBOARD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Visual Actions]
- Show the transition to the full Analyst Command Center.
- Point the camera at the top header displaying the "Live backend" status badge.
- Highlight the 4 main KPI cards at the top of the dashboard:
  * Import Dependency (88%)
  * Hormuz Transit (~42%)
  * SPR Cover (9.5 days)
  * Composite Risk Score (showing current score/100 and primary driver)
- Scroll down to the Corridor Risk Monitor table showing scores, alert levels,
  and volatilities for each corridor.
- Scroll to the Scoring Events Feed showing the latest geopolitical news events.
- ACTION: Click on one of the feed items to expand it, revealing its source
  details and weight composition.

[Narration Script]
"In the Command Center, analysts track real-time threat signals. The Composite 
Risk Index is calculated dynamically based on live news events and risk scores 
across India's supply routes. From here, we can select individual corridors to 
drill down into the underlying intelligence."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 3: RISK INTELLIGENCE PAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Visual Actions]
- ACTION: Click on "Risk Intelligence" in the left sidebar.
- Scroll through the corridor details, demonstrating the confidence metrics 
  and source articles that contribute to the current risk score.

[Narration Script]
"The Risk Intelligence page exposes the raw data driving these scores, 
compiling news feeds, confidence signals, and event weightings to give 
policy-makers a clear audit trail."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 4: SCENARIO MODELLER (THE CORE SIMULATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Visual Actions]
- ACTION: Click on "Scenario Modeller" in the left sidebar. Note the visual
  transition to the premium dark-mode interface.
- Highlight the inputs panel on the left: Sliders for Risk Score, Shock 
  Duration, and Economic Elasticity Assumptions.
- ACTION: Click the "Hormuz Closure (Severe)" preset in the left rail. Show
  the sliders automatically snapping to their preset values.
- Record the animation of the Projected Brent Crude Path fan chart as the
  Monte Carlo simulation runs, displaying the P10, P50, and P90 price paths.
- Scroll down to record the three bottom cards:
  * Pump Price Impact bar chart
  * GDP Impact Range levels
  * Final-Day price distribution histogram
- ACTION: Click the "PDF" export button at the top-right to download the
  scenario brief.

[Narration Script]
"Under the Scenario Modeller, we simulate the financial shocks of physical 
supply disruptions. Selecting the 'Hormuz Closure' preset runs a Monte Carlo 
simulation across 4,000 runs, plotting the P10, P50, and P90 price path 
distributions alongside pump price and macroeconomic GDP forecasts. We can 
then export this brief directly as a PDF."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 5: STRATEGIC RESERVES (SPR) OPTIMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Visual Actions]
- ACTION: Click on "Strategic Reserves" in the left sidebar.
- Highlight the left rail constraints sliders: Planning Horizon, Safety Floor,
  and Max Daily Drawdown.
- Point the camera at the "Value Optimized vs. Baseline" card showing computed
  savings in millions of dollars and percentage improvements.
- Record the Composed Chart showing the optimal drawdown trajectory (Area plot
  for Reserve Level staying above the safety floor, Bar chart for daily
  drawdown, and Line chart for price forecast).
- Scroll down to the day-by-day release table.
- ACTION: Click the "Export CSV" button.

[Narration Script]
"Next is the Strategic Reserves Optimizer. Using the median price path from 
our simulator, an LP solver calculates the most cost-efficient daily drawdown 
schedule. It front-loads releases to avoid purchasing crude during peak price 
spikes while strictly respecting safety floor constraints."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 6: PROCUREMENT & ALTERNATIVE ROUTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Visual Actions]
- ACTION: Click on "Procurement" in the left sidebar (view returns to light mode).
- Point to the Optimization Weights sliders: Cost Efficiency, Security/Risk,
  and Transit Speed.
- ACTION: Drag the Security/Risk slider to 80% and show the "Recalculating..."
  status spinner.
- Record the SVG world map: note how the blue solid line reroutes alternative
  paths (e.g., around the Cape of Good Hope) while the gray dashed line marks
  the original Strait of Hormuz baseline.
- Focus on the Ranked Alternatives list on the right, showing alternative
  suppliers, crude grade compatibility, estimated costs, and reasoning text.
- ACTION: Click the "PDF" export button on the ranked list.

[Narration Script]
"The Procurement module determines optimal alternative routing. By adjusting 
our optimization weights—for instance, prioritizing Security over Cost—the 
NetworkX pathing algorithm penalizes risk corridors. It computes alternative 
routes, such as detouring around the Cape of Good Hope, and ranks backup 
suppliers by compatibility and cost."
