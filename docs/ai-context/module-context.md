# Module Context & Inter-Agent Orchestration

**Orchestrator**: `api.py` `_coordinate()`

## Inter-Agent Data Flow

The Pravah system is designed as a sequence of connected intelligence modules. In production, this flow is executed by the Coordinator (`POST /final-recommendation`):

1.  **Risk Engine (`_run_risk_pipeline`)**:
    *   **Input**: Corridor name (e.g., `hormuz`).
    *   **Process**: Fetches fixtures and/or GDELT live news events. Classifies relevance using regex heuritsics. Assigns severity. Calculates a final risk score (0-100).
    *   **Output**: Risk Score.
2.  **Scenario Engine (`_simulate`)**:
    *   **Input**: Risk Score (from Risk Engine), Corridor.
    *   **Process**: Uses the risk score to modify the parameters of a Monte Carlo simulation (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck log process). The `sigma` (volatility) and `theta` (mean reversion) are scaled by the risk factor.
    *   **Output**: Projected Brent crude price distribution, daily price path, and GDP/Pump impact.
3.  **Procurement Agent (`_recommend`)**:
    *   **Input**: Risk Score.
    *   **Process**: Modifies the `corridor_risk_score` parameter in its NetworkX supply chain graph. Searches for the shortest/most efficient alternative routes and normalizes them into a composite score using Cost, Risk, and Transit time weights.
    *   **Output**: Alternative supplier recommendations.
4.  **SPR Optimizer (`_spr_schedule`)**:
    *   **Input**: Daily price forecasts (from Scenario Engine) + Daily risk scores.
    *   **Process**: Solves a knapsack LP problem using a greedy allocation strategy. Draws down the Strategic Petroleum Reserve on the most expensive/risky days.
    *   **Output**: Optimal daily drawdown schedule and cost savings.

## Reasoning Flows

The system relies on determinism over LLM unpredictability. Although there is a `GEMINI_API_KEY` option for classification in the risk engine, the primary logic is mathematical (Monte Carlo, NetworkX traversal, Greedy LP). 
The final `reasoning_trail` generated by the Coordinator is a concatenated summary of the mathematical outputs, explaining *why* a specific tradeoff was accepted (e.g., "accepted +$2.50/bbl cost for -10 corridor-risk points").
